In 1994, a small group of forward-thinking individuals came together with a shared vision: to create a financial institution that would serve the needs of its community, putting people before profits and offering affordable financial services for all. What started as a simple idea has now grown into a thriving credit union that has been serving members for over three decades.

The Vision: A Community-Centered Financial Cooperative

It all began in the heart of a close-knit community, where members were seeking alternatives to the high fees and impersonal service of traditional banks. A group of local residents, many of whom were workers at nearby businesses and community leaders, recognized that the financial system in their area was not meeting their needs. In particular, they were frustrated by rising interest rates, limited access to credit, and the lack of personalized support.

Our founders, banker Ray Jackson with his wife and accountant Cecilia Jackson, decided to take action. They established Crimson Credit Union based on the cooperative principles of mutual benefit, where every member would have an equal say in the institution's decisions, no matter how much money they had in their accounts. The idea was simple: create a financial institution that focused on people, not profit, and offer financial products with better rates, lower fees, and a more personalized approach.

Crimson Credit Union's commitment to community development went beyond just financial services. It became an active participant in local charities, educational initiatives, and sponsorships that helped improve the quality of life for everyone in the area.

Like many businesses, the credit union faced challenges along the way—economic downturns, regulatory changes, and the rise of online banking. But through it all, the credit union stayed true to its founding principles: to provide its members with affordable, trustworthy, and accessible financial services. During the 2008 financial crisis, while many larger financial institutions faltered, the credit union remained stable, thanks to its cooperative model and its focus on sound financial practices.

In recent years, the credit union has embraced technological advancements, offering online banking and mobile apps to make managing accounts easier for members. Yet, despite these changes, the credit union has never lost sight of its commitment to personalized service and its community-centered approach.

Celebrating 30 Years: A Legacy of Trust 2024

Today, as we celebrate 30 years of service, we reflect on how far we’ve come. From a small group of founders with a vision to a full-fledged credit union with thousands of members, we’ve seen tremendous growth. But through it all, one thing has remained constant: our commitment to the people we serve.

We’ve helped members achieve their financial goals, whether it was buying a home, starting a business, or managing day-to-day expenses. We’ve provided financial education to empower individuals and families to make informed decisions. And, above all, we’ve built a lasting legacy of trust, service, and community.

As we look forward to the next 30 years, we remain dedicated to our founding mission. Our goal is to continue helping members navigate life’s financial journey—offering personalized service, competitive rates, and a sense of belonging that sets us apart from other financial institutions.

Our Board

The Board plays a critical role in setting strategic goals, approving budgets, and ensuring the credit union complies with regulations. They also make key decisions about services, policies, and financial stability. The Board works closely with the credit union’s management team to guide the organization towards long-term success while maintaining the cooperative principles of transparency, fairness, and community focus. As a member-driven organization, credit unions value the input of their members, and the Board serves as the voice of the membership, upholding the mission of the credit union to improve the financial well-being of its members and the community.

A Legacy of Service and Empowerment

Ray Jackson founded Crimson Credit Union in 1994, with the dream of providing accessible, affordable financial services to people who had long been overlooked by traditional banking institutions. His belief in community, financial literacy, and mutual support led to the creation of a credit union that would be both a financial partner and a community resource for everyone, regardless of background or income level.

The passing of Ray Jackson, the beloved founder of Crimson Credit Union, marks the end of an era for our community. Ray was a dedicated, community-driven leader whose vision and tireless efforts brought financial services to those who needed them most. Through his hard work and commitment, he established a credit union that prioritized people over profits and fostered financial empowerment for generations. Ray’s legacy of compassion, service, and leadership will live on in the countless lives he touched. His passing leaves a deep void, but his spirit continues to guide the values and mission of Crimson Credit Union, ensuring his impact will be felt for years to come.

The Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship: Continuing Ray's Vision

The Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship has been established to support collegiate students who share Ray’s values of community involvement, financial empowerment, and personal growth. This scholarship is intended to assist individuals pursuing higher education in fields related to finance, business, or community service, reflecting Ray’s lifelong commitment to building stronger, more informed communities.

The scholarship will be awarded annually to a deserving student who exemplifies the qualities that Ray Jackson held dear—dedication to education, a desire to make a positive impact, and a commitment to helping others achieve financial security.
