Crimson Credit Union Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect a variety of personal information to provide you with financial services, process transactions, and improve your overall experience. This may include:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, address, date of birth, Social Security number (or equivalent), email address, phone number.
  • Financial Information: Account numbers, transaction histories, loan and mortgage details, payment records, balances, and other financial data necessary for service provision.
  • Identification and Verification Data: Driver's license numbers, passport details, or other government-issued IDs.
  • Online Activity Information: IP addresses, browser information, cookies, and browsing data when you access our website or use our online banking services.
  • Third-Party Information: Information from credit bureaus, identity verification services, or other third parties to assess creditworthiness or prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

The personal and financial information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Services: Process your applications for loans, savings accounts, credit cards, and other services. We also use this information to manage and maintain your accounts and transactions.
  • To Improve Our Services: Use your information to personalize your experience, improve our website, and offer tailored financial advice or products.
  • Fraud Prevention and Security: To protect against fraudulent activities and ensure the security of your personal data and financial transactions.
  • Marketing and Communication: With your consent, we may send you marketing materials, offers, or promotions that may be of interest to you. You can opt-out of these communications at any time.
  • Compliance with Legal Requirements: To comply with applicable laws and regulations, including anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) regulations.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your personal and financial information seriously and employ a variety of technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your data. These include:

  • Encryption: We use encryption technology to protect your data, especially during online transactions and when you access our online banking services.
  • Access Controls: We limit access to your information to authorized personnel only, and all employees undergo training in data security and privacy policies.
  • Secure Systems: Our systems undergo regular security assessments to protect against unauthorized access, data breaches, or other potential vulnerabilities.
  • Physical Security: Sensitive information stored on physical media is kept in secure, access-controlled areas.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share your data with third-party service providers that assist us in operating our business (e.g., payment processors, marketing agencies, IT support). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and are not authorized to use your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your personal information if required by law, such as in response to a subpoena, court order, or government investigation.
  • Business Transactions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of any such transfer before it takes place.

We will never sell your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website and online banking services may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These technologies help us understand how you interact with our website, remember your preferences, and improve our services.

  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device that help us remember your login details, preferences, and browsing behavior.
  • Web Beacons: Small transparent images that track how users interact with the website.

You can manage cookie settings through your browser settings, but please note that disabling cookies may affect your experience on our website.

6. Your Rights and Choices

As a member, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and Correction: You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and request corrections if any data is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Data Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal and operational exceptions.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing: If you no longer wish to receive marketing communications from us, you can opt out by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails or contacting us directly.
  • Do Not Track: Some web browsers allow users to send "Do Not Track" signals to websites. While we respect your privacy preferences, please note that we may still collect certain data for functionality and security purposes.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information as soon as possible.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or services. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website or through other communication channels. The updated policy will be effective as of the date it is posted, and your continued use of our services after the update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
