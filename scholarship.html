The Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship: Continuing Ray's Vision

Ray Jackson founded Crimson Credit Union in 1994, with the dream of providing accessible, affordable financial services to people who had long been overlooked by traditional banking institutions. His belief in community, financial literacy, and mutual support led to the creation of a credit union that would be both a financial partner and a community resource for everyone, regardless of background or income level.

Ray's passion was not just about creating a financial institution; it was about empowering individuals to take control of their financial futures. He believed that by offering education, support, and resources, people could break the cycle of financial uncertainty and achieve long-term stability and success.

The Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship has been established to support collegiate students who share Ray’s values of community involvement, financial empowerment, and personal growth. This scholarship is intended to assist individuals pursuing higher education in fields related to finance, business, or community service, reflecting Ray’s lifelong commitment to building stronger, more informed communities.

The scholarship will be awarded annually to a deserving student who exemplifies the qualities that Ray Jackson held dear—dedication to education, a desire to make a positive impact, and a commitment to helping others achieve financial security.

Ray Jackson was a visionary whose core values centered around community, financial literacy, education, volunteer work, and charity. He believed that a strong community is built on the foundation of mutual support, where individuals come together to lift each other up. His commitment to financial literacy was at the heart of his work, as he wanted to empower people with the knowledge to make informed decisions, secure their financial futures, and break free from cycles of financial uncertainty.

Ray was also a staunch advocate for education, seeing it as the key to personal growth and long-term success. He supported lifelong learning and was passionate about ensuring that others had the resources and opportunities to pursue their educational goals. His dedication to volunteer work was a testament to his belief in giving back, as he actively engaged in community projects and encouraged others to do the same.

Above all, Ray’s values were rooted in charity — the idea that helping others, whether through financial support, education, or time, is essential to building a more equitable and compassionate society. These principles continue to guide the work of the credit union he founded and inspire the community he helped build.

Eligibility Criteria

To be considered for the Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Academic Excellence: A minimum GPA of 3.5 on a 4.0-point scale.
  • Field of Study: Preference will be given to students pursuing a degree in finance, business administration, economics, or a related field. However, students planning to pursue careers in nonprofit management, social work, or community development will also be considered.
  • Community Involvement: Applicants should demonstrate a strong record of community service or volunteer work, showcasing Ray Jackson’s belief in the importance of giving back to others.
  • Essay/Statement: Applicants must submit a personal essay (500-750 words) that discusses their career aspirations, how they plan to contribute to their community, and how Ray Jackson’s values have influenced their goals.
  • Financial Need: The scholarship will prioritize applicants who demonstrate financial need and could benefit from additional educational support.

Award Details

The scholarship offers a one-time award of $10,000, which can be applied toward tuition, textbooks, and other educational expenses. In addition to the monetary award, the recipient will receive recognition at the annual Crimson Credit Union member event and be featured in our newsletters and on our website.

How to Apply

To apply for the Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship, applicants should:

  1. Complete the scholarship application form available here.
  2. Submit their most recent academic transcript.
  3. Provide a letter of recommendation from a teacher, mentor, or community leader who can speak to their character, academic achievements, and community involvement.
  4. Submit their personal essay addressing the essay prompt above.
  5. Applications are due by April 20, and the winner will be notified by June 1.

Ray's Lasting Impact

Ray Jackson's dedication to fostering financial independence, his passion for education, and his commitment to community will live on through this scholarship. Each year, the Ray Jackson Memorial Scholarship will honor his memory by supporting individuals who strive to make a difference, just as Ray did throughout his life.

We are proud to offer this opportunity to young people who are eager to follow in Ray's footsteps and continue the work of creating strong, financially empowered communities.
